# Prompt Log — About Section Content

## 1. My Prompt

Context: I am a second-year Software Engineering student building a one-page
personal portfolio website. I am interested in front-end development,
problem-solving, and I enjoy hiking and cooking in my free time.

Goal: Write a short, professional "About Me" bio for the About section of my
portfolio, plus one short tagline for my header.

Constraints: Keep the bio to 3–4 sentences, use a friendly but professional
tone, avoid generic buzzwords such as "passionate go-getter", and mention that
I am eager to grow as a developer.

Format: Give me the tagline on one line, then the bio as a separate paragraph.

## 2. AI's Raw Output

Tagline: "Passionate Front-End Developer in the Making"

Bio: "I am a passionate and driven Software Engineering student with a keen
interest in front-end development. I love solving problems and building
creative digital experiences. In my free time, I enjoy hiking and cooking,
which help me stay balanced and inspired. I am eager to grow my skills and
become a well-rounded developer."

## 3. My Final Edited Version

Tagline: "Turning ideas into interactive web experiences."

Bio: "I'm a second-year Software Engineering student who genuinely enjoys
turning a blank page into a working website. Front-end development is where
I feel most creative, and I'm currently sharpening my HTML, CSS and JavaScript
skills. Outside of coding, you'll usually find me hiking a local trail or
experimenting in the kitchen."

## 4. Reflection

I changed the tagline because the AI's version felt generic and overused.
The phrase "passionate... in the making" sounded like a template rather than
something personal. I rewrote the bio to remove generic wording such as
"passionate and driven" and added the more specific idea of turning a blank
page into a working website. I also adjusted the hobbies sentence so the final
version sounds more natural and more like my own voice.
